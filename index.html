New for 2027

Three keepers per team in 2027

We started with one keeper this year to learn the format. For 2027, every team can keep up to three players from its end-of-season roster.

  • Every player on every roster is priced below — what he would cost you as a 2027 keeper.
  • Rosters are pulled from Sleeper and will keep changing until the season ends. Your keepers come from your roster as it stands at the end of 2026.
  • Players kept in 2026 go up in price if you keep them a second time — see “The Escalator” below.

Injured Reserve (IR) slot

Each team gets one IR spot, so a player who lands on NFL injured reserve can be stashed instead of dropped. It only accepts a true IR or PUP designation — a week-to-week Out or Doubtful tag won't fit. This allows you to keep an injured player for next year, if you like.

2027 Keeper Rules and Rosters

2027 Keepers

How keepers work

Keep up to three players from your 2026 end-of-season roster.

Up from one keeper this year. Keepers are optional — keep three, two, one or none.

Each keeper costs a pick in the 2027 draft.

Keeping a player means giving up that round’s pick.

The keeper price is where the player was drafted last year.

Some nuances:

  • Players picked up on waivers who were undrafted cost a 15th-round pick.
  • Players picked up on waivers who had been drafted cost the round they were originally drafted in.
  • Two of your keepers can't cost the same round. If two would both be a 15th, the second one moves up to a 14th.
  • A traded player keeps his price too — it follows the player, not the team.

The price goes up one round every year you keep the same player.

  • This is known as “The Escalator.”
  • Your first year keeping a player, the player costs exactly the round they were drafted in. Each year after that, the cost moves up one round: a 10th becomes a 9th, then an 8th, and so on.
  • Once the price would pass round 1, the player can no longer be kept and re-enters the draft.

What you need to do.

  1. Find your team below and pick up to three keepers. The 2027 cost of each player is labelled (R1, R10, etc).
  2. Post your keeper choices in the Sleeper app league chat (or email Ben) before the deadline.
  3. If you don’t want keepers or you miss the deadline, no big deal! You’ll get all your picks in the draft.
